About Calc71

Calc71 is a free collection of online calculators for everyday needs: BMI, age, percentages and loan EMI. Each tool comes with a plain-language guide, so you can see the formula and understand the result instead of just trusting a number.

Our aim is simple: accurate tools, clear explanations and no clutter. The site is funded by advertising, which lets us keep every calculator free.

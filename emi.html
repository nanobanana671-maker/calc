Loan EMI Calculator

Work out your monthly instalment, total interest and total repayment before you borrow.

How EMI works

An equated monthly instalment (EMI) is the fixed amount you pay each month until a loan is cleared. Early payments contain mostly interest, and later payments contain mostly principal. The formula is EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Worked example

For a loan of 1,000,000 at 12% a year over 5 years, r is 0.01 and n is 60. The EMI comes to about 22,244. Over 60 months you repay about 1,334,600, so the interest cost is roughly 334,600.

How to lower your EMI

A longer tenure reduces the monthly amount but increases total interest. A larger down payment, a lower interest rate or a part-prepayment can all reduce what you pay overall. Compare offers from several lenders, and check for processing fees, early-repayment charges and insurance add-ons, which this calculator does not include.

Frequently asked questions

Is the interest reducing balance or flat?

This calculator uses the reducing balance method, which most banks use. Flat-rate loans cost more for the same stated rate.

Does the result include fees?

No. Fees and taxes charged by your lender are extra, so confirm the final figures with them.

Can I use it for car, home or personal loans?

Yes. Any loan with a fixed rate and equal monthly payments follows the same formula.

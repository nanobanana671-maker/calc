What is BMI?

Body mass index is a number that compares your weight with your height. It was designed as a quick screening tool for groups of people, and it is widely used by doctors as a first check. The formula is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

How to calculate it

Take a person who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm (1.70 m) tall. Height squared is 1.70 × 1.70 = 2.89. Divide 70 by 2.89 and you get a BMI of 24.2, which sits in the healthy range.

BMI ranges for adults

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25 – 29.9Overweight
30 and aboveObese

Limits of BMI

BMI does not measure body fat directly. Muscular people can score as overweight even with low body fat, and older adults may have a normal BMI with little muscle. Some research suggests health risks begin at lower BMI values for people of South Asian descent. Waist size, diet, activity level and family history all matter too, so use BMI as one signal and not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for children?

Children and teenagers are assessed with growth charts that compare BMI with others of the same age and sex. This calculator is for adults aged 18 and over.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is considered the healthy range.

How can I improve my BMI?

Steady changes work best: balanced meals, regular movement, enough sleep and gradual weight change. Ask your doctor before starting any major diet or exercise plan.

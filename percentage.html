Percentage Calculator

Three common percentage questions answered instantly.

1. What is X% of Y?
2. X is what percent of Y?
3. Percentage change

Percentage formulas

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. To find what percent X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. For percentage change, subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

Worked examples

20% of 150 is 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30. The number 30 is 25% of 120 because 30 ÷ 120 = 0.25. A price rising from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase, since the change of 20 divided by the original 80 equals 0.25.

Everyday uses

Percentages appear in discounts, sales tax, tips, exam marks, interest rates and salary raises. A 30% discount on a 2,000 item saves 600, so you pay 1,400. Remember that a rise of 50% followed by a fall of 50% does not return you to the start, because the second percentage applies to a larger number.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If an interest rate moves from 4% to 6%, it rose by 2 percentage points, which is a 50% increase in the rate itself.

Can the result be negative?

Yes. In the change calculator a negative result means the value decreased.
